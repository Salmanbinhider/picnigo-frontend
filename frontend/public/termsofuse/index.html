3. How to report objectionable content

Every user can report content or behavior they find offensive directly inside the Picnigo app. Reports are sent to our moderation team for review.

Report a post

  1. Open the post you want to report.
  2. Tap the more options menu (⋯) on the post.
  3. Select Report.
  4. Choose a reason (e.g. nudity, hate speech, bullying, spam).
  5. Confirm — you will see: “Report submitted. Our team will review it.”

Report a comment

  1. Open the comments on a post.
  2. Tap the more options menu on the comment.
  3. Select Report and choose a reason.

Report a chat or user in messages

  1. Open the conversation in Chat.
  2. Tap the more options menu at the top of the chat.
  3. Select Report and choose a reason.

You may also email us directly at info@picnigo.com with details about the concern.

4. How to block abusive users

Picnigo lets you block other users so they cannot interact with you through direct messages and related features.

Block from a chat

  1. Open the conversation with the user.
  2. Tap the more options menu.
  3. Select Block and confirm.

Manage blocked users

  1. Go to your Profile tab.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Blocked to view or unblock users.

When you block someone, you will no longer receive direct messages from that user. You can unblock them at any time from Settings > Blocked.
