Child Safety Standards

Picnigo — available on the Google Play Store

Last updated: July 15, 2026

These Child Safety Standards apply to Picnigo, the social travel application published on the Google Play Store, and to all services operated by the Picnigo team. We are committed to providing a safe environment for all of our users and to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation on our platform.

Picnigo strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We have zero tolerance for any content, behavior, or activity that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or abuses children.

1. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

The following are strictly prohibited on Picnigo and will result in immediate removal of content, permanent account termination, and reporting to the relevant authorities:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or possessing any visual, written, or audio content that depicts the sexual abuse or exploitation of a minor, whether real, edited, or computer-generated.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a child with the intent to sexually abuse or exploit them, including building trust for later abusive purposes.
  • Sextortion: threatening or blackmailing a child using real or alleged access to intimate images of that child.
  • Sexualization of minors: any content or behavior that depicts, encourages, or promotes the sexual abuse, sexualization, or exploitation of children.
  • Child trafficking: advertising or soliciting a child for commercial sexual exploitation.
  • Any other conduct that endangers the safety or wellbeing of a minor.

2. Age Requirements

Picnigo is intended for users aged 13 and older. Accounts found to belong to users under the minimum age, or accounts misrepresenting a user's age, may be suspended or removed.

3. In-App Reporting

Picnigo provides in-app tools that allow any user to report content, accounts, or messages that they believe violate these standards. Reports concerning child safety are treated with the highest priority, reviewed by our moderation team, and acted on promptly.

4. Content Moderation and Enforcement

When we become aware of content or behavior that violates these standards, we will:

  • Immediately remove the violating content from the platform;
  • Permanently terminate the accounts involved;
  • Preserve relevant evidence as required by applicable law; and
  • Report confirmed Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and related activity to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or relevant local and national law enforcement agencies.

5. Compliance with Child Safety Laws

Picnigo complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations in the regions where our app is available, and we cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations relating to child sexual abuse and exploitation, in accordance with applicable law.

6. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter content or behavior on Picnigo that you believe involves the abuse or exploitation of a child, please report it immediately using the in-app reporting tools or by contacting our child safety team directly:

Child Safety Contact — Picnigo Team

Email: info@picnigo.com (subject line: “Child Safety Report”)

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact your local law enforcement or emergency services right away. You can also report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org or to the relevant authority in your country.

7. Updates to These Standards

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time. The latest version will always be published at this page. Continued use of Picnigo after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised standards.
