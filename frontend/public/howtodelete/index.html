How to Delete Your Picnigo Account

App: Picnigo

Entity: PICNIGO LLP

Last Updated: 24 April 2026

Delete Account from the App

  1. Open the Picnigo app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to your profile or account settings page.
  3. Tap Delete My Account.
  4. Follow the confirmation prompts to complete deletion.

What Happens After Deletion Request

After your request is submitted, your account is deleted and personal data is removed or anonymized, except information we are required to retain for legal, security, fraud prevention, or accounting purposes.

If you are not able to delete your account through the app, please contact info@picnigo.com for support.
